## Prompt (124 words)

In the 1995 final environmental impact statement on the operation of Glen Canyon Dam whose preferred alternative was the Modified Low Fluctuating Flow Alternative, the Chapter IV environmental-consequences analysis of sediment impacts presents a table of sand transport capacity and probability of net gain in sand storage between the Paria River and the Little Colorado River for a minimum release year. That discussion also states that habitat maintenance flows in a minimum release year generally would decrease the probability of net gain in sand storage by about 11 percent. Setting aside that habitat-maintenance difference and the No Action Alternative value, what exact probability of net gain in sand storage does that table assign to the Modified Low Fluctuating Flow Alternative? Report the percentage only.

## Failure justification

The model answered **64%** and cited Chapter 3 (`chap3-1.pdf`). That is the wrong chapter: Table IV-7 sits in **Chapter IV Environmental Consequences** (`Chap4.pdf`, printed p.195), not Chapter III Affected Environment. On Table IV-7, **64** is the probability of net gain in sand storage for the **Seasonally adjusted steady flow** alternative, not for the preferred **Modified Low Fluctuating Flow Alternative**, whose probability cell is **59**. The same page’s searchable distractor is the habitat-maintenance prose **about 11 percent** (table difference **-11**), and No Action is **47**. The model never locked the MLFF row on the Chapter 4 consequences table, so it missed **59%**.
