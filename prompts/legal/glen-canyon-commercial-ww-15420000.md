## Failure justification

The model answered **735,000**. That value is selectable on the same Table III-16 page, but it is the **Local economic activity generated** figure for **commercial white-water boating below Diamond Creek**, not for commercial white-water boating **in Grand Canyon**. The Grand Canyon commercial row’s local-economic-activity cell is **15,420,000** (direct expenditures on that same row are **9,581,000**; the table total local activity is **23,115,000**, matching the prose “approximately $23 million” distractor). The model found the right table and column family but took the wrong commercial white-water row, so it missed the FEIS Grand Canyon commercial local-activity value.
