## Prompt (134 words)

In the 1995 final environmental impact statement on the operation of Glen Canyon Dam, whose preferred alternative was the Modified Low Fluctuating Flow Alternative, the Chapter IV environmental-consequences analysis of hydropower impacts evaluates effects on the marketable resource available for firm power marketing under the Salt Lake City Area Integrated Projects. That discussion also states that under the Interim Low Fluctuating Flow Alternative, the marketable resource available for firm power marketing would decrease by 372 MW of capacity in winter compared to the No Action Alternative. Setting aside that Interim Low Fluctuating Flow winter figure and any summer-season values, by how many megawatts of capacity would the marketable resource available for firm power marketing be reduced in winter under the Modified Low Fluctuating Flow Alternative compared to no action? Report the integer megawatts only.
