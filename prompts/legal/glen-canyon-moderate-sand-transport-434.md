## Prompt (132 words)

In the 1995 final environmental impact statement on the operation of Glen Canyon Dam, the Chapter IV sediment analysis presents a table of sand transport capacity and probability of net gain in sand storage between the Paria River and the Little Colorado River for a minimum release year. That discussion also states that habitat maintenance flows in a minimum release year generally would result in a net increase in sand transport capacity of about 30 percent, and the same table assigns the Modified Low Fluctuating Flow Alternative a sand transport capacity of 424 thousand tons. Setting aside that habitat-maintenance percentage, the Modified Low Fluctuating Flow capacity, and the probability-of-net-gain column, what exact sand transport capacity in thousand tons does that table assign to the Moderate Fluctuating Flow Alternative? Report the integer only.
