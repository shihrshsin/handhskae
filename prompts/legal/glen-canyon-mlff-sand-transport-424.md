## Prompt (118 words)

In the 1995 final environmental impact statement on the operation of Glen Canyon Dam whose preferred alternative was the Modified Low Fluctuating Flow Alternative, the Chapter IV sediment analysis presents a table of sand transport capacity and probability of net gain in sand storage between the Paria River and the Little Colorado River for a minimum release year. That discussion also states that habitat maintenance flows in a minimum release year generally would result in a net increase in sand transport capacity of about 30 percent. Setting aside that habitat-maintenance percentage and the probability-of-net-gain column, what exact sand transport capacity in thousand tons does that table assign to the Modified Low Fluctuating Flow Alternative? Report the integer only.
