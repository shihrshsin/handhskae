## Prompt (78 words)

In the Bureau of Reclamation's 1995 final environmental impact statement on the operation of Glen Canyon Dam, whose preferred alternative was the Modified Low Fluctuating Flow Alternative, the recreation chapter includes a bar chart of mooring quality under constant high, constant low, and fluctuating releases. Later analysis states that mooring would be fair to good at 92 percent of camping beaches under steady flow alternatives. On that mooring-quality chart, what exact Fair/Good percentage is shown for fluctuating releases?
