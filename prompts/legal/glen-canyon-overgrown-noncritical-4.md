## Prompt (87 words)

In the 1995 final environmental impact statement on the operation of Glen Canyon Dam whose preferred alternative was the Modified Low Fluctuating Flow Alternative, the text notes that vegetation encroachment accounted for nearly 50 percent of campsite degradation in wider noncritical reaches and that erosion was the primary cause of campsite degradation in narrow critical reaches. Setting aside that nearly-50-percent remark and every Non-Critical slice except Overgrown, what exact percentage does the Non-Critical Reaches pie in the figure those sentences cite assign to the Overgrown category alone?
