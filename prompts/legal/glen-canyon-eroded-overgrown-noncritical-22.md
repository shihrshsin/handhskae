## Prompt (81 words)

In the Bureau of Reclamation's 1995 final environmental impact statement on the operation of Glen Canyon Dam, whose preferred alternative was the Modified Low Fluctuating Flow Alternative, the recreation chapter includes a two-pie figure on the number of camps degraded by reach type and type of degradation. Setting aside the body-text remark that vegetation encroachment accounted for nearly 50 percent of campsite degradation in wider noncritical reaches, what exact percentage does the Non-Critical Reaches pie assign to the combined Eroded/Overgrown category?
