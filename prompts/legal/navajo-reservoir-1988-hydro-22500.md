# Legal — 1988/89 Navajo Reservoir hydrologic determination (perpetuity increment)

**Answer:** `22500` (or `22,500`)  
**Famous twins:** `6.0` MAF Upper Basin yield (searchable in the 2007 HD update); `94,500` acre-feet/year total Navajo marketing block (UCRC resolution / same-doc summary). Secondary: `69,000` (through 2039), `3,000` (Jicarilla perpetuity).

---

## Prompt (87 words)

In February 1989 the Acting Secretary of the Interior signed a hydrologic determination—required under Section 11(a) of the 1962 statute authorizing the Navajo Indian Irrigation and San Juan-Chama Projects—concerning water available from Navajo Reservoir for long-term municipal and industrial contracts in New Mexico. Beyond the block previously identified in 1984 for marketing only through 2039, and aside from a small tribal reservation in perpetuity, how many additional acre-feet per year did that determination find were reasonably likely to be available for depletion from Navajo Reservoir in perpetuity?

---

## Answer

`22500`

**Step 4 — Fetch (twin):** http://www.riversimulator.org/Resources/USBR/2007HydrologicDetermination.pdf — **Comparison to 1988 Hydrologic Determination** (text layer): *“The 1988 Hydrologic Determination concluded that the total Upper Basin yield, including CRSP reservoir evaporation, is at least **6.0** maf per year…”* — headline twin; not the Navajo perpetuity increment.

**Step 5 — Fetch (answer):** 1988 PDF, **page 23** (printed p. 20), section **“VI. Determination”**: *“…depletions up to **94,500** acre-feet annually. Of this amount, **3,000** … Jicarilla Apache Tribe, **69,000** … through the year 2039, and an additional **22,500** acre-feet of water annually is reasonably likely to be available for depletion from Navajo Reservoir in perpetuity.”*

**Step 6 — Confirm:** Same split on **PDF page 4**, **“I. Executive Summary”** (also image-only): additional **22,500** acre-feet/year available from Navajo Reservoir in perpetuity.

**Step 7 — Verify:** Answer is **22,500** (the perpetuity increment beyond the 69,000 through-2039 block and the 3,000 tribal reserve)—not **6.0** MAF and not the total **94,500**.
