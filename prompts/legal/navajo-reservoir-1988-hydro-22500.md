# Legal — 1988/89 Navajo Reservoir hydrologic determination (perpetuity increment)

**Answer:** `22500`  
**Famous twins:** `6.0` MAF Upper Basin yield; `94500` total Navajo marketing block. Secondary: `69000` (through 2039), `3000` (Jicarilla).  
**Observed model failure:** Hallucinated `20200`.

---

## Prompt (87 words)

In February 1989 the Acting Secretary of the Interior signed a hydrologic determination—required under Section 11(a) of the 1962 statute authorizing the Navajo Indian Irrigation and San Juan-Chama Projects—concerning water available from Navajo Reservoir for long-term municipal and industrial contracts in New Mexico. Beyond the block previously identified in 1984 for marketing only through 2039, and aside from a small tribal reservation in perpetuity, how many additional acre-feet per year did that determination find were reasonably likely to be available for depletion from Navajo Reservoir in perpetuity?

---

## Answer

`22500`
