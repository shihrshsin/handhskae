## Prompt (73 words)

In the mid-1990s Johnson & Johnson sought U.S. clearance to put a 1% ketoconazole dandruff shampoo on ordinary drugstore shelves under the Nizoral A-D name. The environmental assessment filed with that application estimates how much leftover bulk shampoo would load wastewater at the contract plant.
